I am currently a 5-th year Ph.D. student at [C.N.Yang Institute for Theoretical Physics](http://insti.physics.sunysb.edu/itp/www/), under the supervision of [Prof. Tzu-Chieh Wei](http://insti.physics.sunysb.edu/~twei/). Before that I obtained B.S degree in Physics from Department of Physics & Astronomy,  Stony Brook University.  During my undergraduate time,  I worked as student researcher at [Brookaven National Laboratory](https://www.bnl.gov/world/) and [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://www.lanl.gov) as well as [Department of Physics and Astronomy, Stony Brook University](https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/physics/). During my Ph.D. study, I was an intern at [QuEra Computing Inc](https://www.quera.com) (Summer 2025), a visiting student to [Harvard University](https://www.harvard.edu) (Fall 2025-Spring 2026). In the summer of 2026, I  begin to work at [Google Quantum AI](https://quantumai.google). 

My research is centered on the fascinating intersection of quantum physics and computer science, as well as mathematics. I am deeply intrigued by how the principles of quantum physics offer an entirely new paradigm for computing, enabling a new computational framework, namely quantum computing, which can tackle complex computational challenges that pose significant hurdles for classical computers. I am specifically interested in developing quantum algorithms for a wide array of tasks, including scientific computing, machine learning, topological & geometrical data analysis, simulating quantum system, etc. 

I am blessed with amazing family, friends, colleagues, and teachers in my life !
